About this demo. A portfolio MLOps pipeline: containerised FastAPI service serving eight PyTorch checkpoints, behind Caddy reverse proxy with auto-HTTPS, deployed on a single Hetzner VPS with rootless Podman. The focus is the engineering and deployment, not the model.
Research demo only. Not a medical device. Not for clinical use, diagnosis, or treatment decisions. Trained on public research datasets (CheXpert, TBX11K); accuracy may drop sharply on X-rays from other hospitals or equipment. Do not use predictions here for any medical decision. See project notes for details.
